## 1. Overview

Pakistan issues Machine Readable Passports (MRP) and e-Passports through DGI&P. Passports can be applied for at any Regional Passport Office (RPO), Executive Passport Office (EPO), Passport Processing Counter (PPC), or Pakistani mission abroad — applicants are **not** restricted to the office matching their CNIC address (Passport Rule 6, 2021).

Four passport types exist: Ordinary (green, for personal travel), Official (government employees on official business), Diplomatic (diplomats/senior officials), and Executive (senior officials and their families). This knowledge base focuses on the **Ordinary passport**, since that is what the vast majority of citizens need.

## 2. Who Can Apply

- Any Pakistani citizen holding a valid CNIC or NICOP.
- Minors (under 18), through a parent or court-appointed legal guardian.
- Overseas Pakistanis, including for renewal, through the online portal.

## 3. Required Documents

### Adults (18 and above)
1. Proof of passport fee payment — original bank paid fee challan, or e-payment confirmation (receipt/SMS/email) with the PSID number.
2. Original valid CNIC or NICOP, plus one photocopy.
3. Previous passport (original + photocopy), if one was issued before.
4. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the employer, required only for government, semi-government, or autonomous body employees.
5. Foreign passport + photocopy, for dual nationals only.
6. For a lost passport: a police report naming the previous passport number.

### Minors (under 18)
- Proof of fee payment (as above).
- Original valid CNICs or passports of **both** parents, with photocopies.
- The minor must be accompanied by a parent or a court-appointed legal guardian.
- Age 10 and above: an original, updated Child Registration Certificate (CRC) with fingerprints and photo, or a Juvenile Card.
- Under age 10: an original CRC (Form "B") or Family Registration Certificate (FRC).
- If parents are separated/divorced: custody papers from the court, and/or notarized consent from the non-applying parent.
- If a parent is deceased: original death certificate + photocopy.
- Guardians need a court-issued guardianship certificate and must appear in person with the minor.

## 4. Application Process (in-person, at RPO/EPO/PPC or mission abroad)

1. Pay the prescribed fee in advance (bank challan, ATM, internet banking, JazzCash/Easypaisa via 1Bill, or the "Passport Fee Asaan" mobile app / e-payment web portal) and keep the receipt/PSID.
2. Arrive at the Customer Service Counter.
3. Photograph is captured and a token with QR code is issued.
4. Biometrics / fingerprints are captured.
5. Data entry is completed by staff.
6. Records are verified against the Exit Control List / Black List (ECL/BL).
7. An Assistant Director interviews and decides on the case.
8. Any additional fee is paid via QR code if applicable.
9. Passport is collected by the applicant in person (or by a blood relative on the scheduled date) — it will not be released to unrelated third parties.

At offices with a "One Window" facility, steps 2–5 are handled at a single desk.

## 5. Processing Time (after successful submission)

| Priority | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| Fast Track (available in 47 cities) | 2 working days |
| Urgent | 5 working days |
| Normal | 21 working days |

Note: courier transit time to remote areas of Pakistan or to missions abroad is **not** included in these figures.

## 6. Validity

- 5 years or 10 years, applicant's choice (fee differs).
- Children under 15 can only be issued a 5-year passport.

### Lost passport surcharge
- 1st-time loss: double the normal fee.
- 2nd-time loss: quadruple the normal fee.
- 3rd-time loss (for applicants abroad): referred to DGI&P Headquarters for a decision.

## 8. Online / e-Services

- **Online Passport Renewal:** available for both inland and overseas Pakistanis, via https://dgip.gov.pk/eServices/online-passport.php and https://onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk/.
- Overseas applicants can also process: new passport for a newborn (under 2 years), reprint for damage, and reprint for exhausted pages, online.
- Fee can be paid by credit/debit card (Visa/Mastercard) at the final step of the online form.
- Home delivery is currently only available for overseas applicants; inland applicants collect from their preferred RPO/EPO.
- **No refund policy** — once a passport application fee is paid, it is not refunded even if the application is abandoned.

## 9. Renewal, Modification, Lost & Damaged Passports

- **Renewal:** allowed any time before expiry, no restriction; the old passport is returned to the applicant after processing.
- **Modification** (name, father's name, date of birth, etc.): requires the CNIC/NICOP to be corrected *first*, then a passport application with supporting documents. Cases involving multiple changed fields go to DGI&P Islamabad for approval.
- **Lost passport:** file a police report first, then pay the applicable (doubled/quadrupled) fee.
- **Damaged / pages exhausted:** apply for a fresh passport (choice of 36/72/100 pages), fee as per the fee table plus any extra-page surcharge.

## 10. Office Timings

- Most RPOs/EPOs/PPCs: Monday–Friday, 08:00–16:00 (prayer/lunch break 13:00–13:30).
- Selected high-volume offices (e.g., RPO G-10/4 Islamabad, RPO Garden Town Lahore, RPO Awami Markaz Karachi, and NADRA Mega Centers) run **24/7** in three shifts: 08:00–16:00, 16:00–24:00, 24:00–08:00.
- DGI&P offices are closed on public holidays.

## 13. Common Pitfalls (for the agent to proactively warn users about)

- Assuming Fast Track is available everywhere — it's only offered in 47 designated cities.
- Confusing MRP fees with e-Passport fees; they are different fee tables.
- Not knowing that Fast Track is unavailable for e-Passport delivery.
- Forgetting the NOC requirement for government/semi-government employees.
- Believing a passport fee is refundable if the application is withdrawn — it is not.
- Assuming the passport must be collected at the RPO nearest to the CNIC address — it can be collected at any RPO chosen at the time of application.
