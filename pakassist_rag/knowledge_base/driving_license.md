## Overview

Driving licenses in Pakistan are issued separately by each of the five
provinces/territories. The general process (learner permit, test, issuance)
is consistent, but exact fees and online portal names vary by province.

## Eligibility

Minimum age 18 for motorcars, 16 for motorcycles (light). Applicants must
pass a written/computerized test and a practical driving test.

## Required documents

- Valid CNIC
- Learner's permit (issued after passing the written test)
- Medical certificate (in some provinces)
- Passport-size photographs

## Process steps

1. Apply for and pass the learner's permit test.
2. Practice driving for the mandatory learner period.
3. Book and pass the practical driving test.
4. Pay applicable fees and collect the permanent license.

## Fees

Fees differ by province and are not stated here with confidence — see
`sources.md` and confirm against the current provincial notification before
quoting a number to a user.

## Validity / renewal

Typically valid for a set number of years (varies by province) and
renewable before expiry; late renewal may incur a penalty.
